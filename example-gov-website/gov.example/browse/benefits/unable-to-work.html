Benefits and financial support if you're temporarily unable to work

Find financial help if illness or a health condition means you cannot work for a limited period.

Overview

You may be able to get financial support when you are temporarily unable to work. The support available depends on your employment status, earnings, savings, household, and how long your illness lasts.

Tell your employer or the relevant benefit service when your circumstances change, including when you return to work.

Check what you could get

A benefits calculator can estimate the support you may receive. An eligibility checker can also help you understand which claims to consider before you apply.

You may need details of your income, savings, housing costs, household, and recent employment. An estimate is not an award decision.

Benefits and financial support

Different payments cover different circumstances. Check the eligibility rules and whether your employer must pay you before starting a benefit claim.

  • Statutory Sick Pay may be available from your employer for a qualifying period of illness.
  • Employment and Support Allowance may be available if illness or disability affects how much you can work.
  • Universal Credit may help with living and housing costs if your income is reduced.

Evidence and changes

You may be asked for medical evidence, such as a fit note, and information about your work and earnings. Keep copies of anything you submit.

Report changes promptly, including changes to your health, income, household, or ability to work. A delay can affect your payment or create an overpayment.
