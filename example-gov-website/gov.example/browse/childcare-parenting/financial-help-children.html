Financial help if you have children

Find out about benefits and other support for families with children.

Overview

Financial support can help with the cost of raising a child. What you can get may depend on your income, savings, work, immigration status and the children in your care.

Benefits and tax support

You may be able to claim child benefit, universal credit or tax support. Check the eligibility rules and apply as soon as you can.

Support for a new baby or adoption

Some parents and adopters can get payments or leave from work. You may also be eligible for help with essential costs depending on your circumstances.

Help with childcare costs

Support may be available through funded childcare hours, tax-free childcare or a benefits claim. Check whether your chosen provider must be approved.

Report changes

Tell the office responsible for your payment when your income, work, household or childcare costs change. Delays can lead to incorrect payments that may need to be repaid.
