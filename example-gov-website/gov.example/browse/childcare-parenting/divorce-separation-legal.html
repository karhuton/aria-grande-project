Divorce, separation and legal issues

Information about child maintenance, parentage and resolving family disagreements.

Overview

When parents separate, decisions should focus on the child’s welfare. Try to communicate safely and keep a record of important agreements.

Make arrangements for children

Agree where children will live, how they will spend time with each parent and how important decisions will be made. Mediation may help if you cannot agree.

Child maintenance

Child maintenance helps with a child’s everyday living costs. Parents can make a private arrangement or use a statutory service where one is available.

Parentage

Legal parenthood and parental responsibility can affect who makes decisions for a child. Get advice if there is a disagreement or uncertainty about parentage.

Get legal help

Get independent legal advice for complex family matters. If there is a risk of harm, seek urgent help from emergency services or a specialist support organisation.
