Overview

This page explains the main steps, responsibilities and support relating to child benefit. Check the requirements that apply to your circumstances.

What you need to know

Read the relevant guidance before you begin. Keep important documents and details ready, and check any eligibility rules, deadlines or fees.

Apply or take action

Use the appropriate official service to apply, register, report a change or make an appointment. Keep confirmation messages and copies of information you submit.

Get help

Contact the responsible office if you cannot find an answer or need support. Give your reference number when you contact them and do not share passwords or security codes.
